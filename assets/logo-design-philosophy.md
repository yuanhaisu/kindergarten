# Tender Cosmos

A design philosophy for visual artifacts that speak the language of gentle wonder.

## Space and Form

Forms inhabit a gravitational field of care — every shape is rounded, softened, slightly inflated as if breathing. Nothing is sharp; nothing is threatening. Circles and arcs dominate, their curves reminiscent of planetary bodies seen through a child's eyes. The composition centers on a single anchoring form from which smaller elements radiate outward in orbital rhythms, creating a sense of belonging and protected exploration. Negative space is generous but never vacant — it is the quiet sky that makes the stars visible.

## Color and Material

The palette draws from a softened celestial spectrum: a warm cerulean blue that feels like a hug rather than cold space, a tender sprout-green that carries the promise of growth, and amber-orange accents that pulse with gentle warmth. Colors overlap with translucent layering, never opaque blocks. The overall impression is of light passing through stained glass made of clouds — diffuse, hopeful, and impossibly kind.

## Scale and Rhythm

Elements follow a parent-child proportional logic: one dominant sphere establishes the center of gravity, secondary forms cluster at one-third scale, and accent points scatter at one-tenth. This hierarchy mirrors the nurturing relationship — the large protects the small, the small animates the large. Repetition appears in orbital patterns, not rigid grids. Three small stars trace an invisible arc above the planet, their irregular placement suggesting natural constellation rather than mechanical arrangement.

## Composition and Balance

The composition is asymmetric but stable — the primary form sits slightly below center, anchored by a growth element reaching upward and rightward. This diagonal tension between rootedness and aspiration is the central narrative. Stars balance the visual weight on the opposite diagonal. The result feels both grounded and airborne, a planet that is simultaneously home and launch point.

## Craftsmanship

Every curve has been redrawn until it feels inevitable. Gradients are calibrated with painstaking precision — no banding, no harsh transitions, only the seamless modulation of a master colorist. Edge softness varies deliberately: the planet's rim is crisp against the void, while internal glow effects feather into transparency. This is not computer-generated geometry; it is the product of deep expertise, each vertex nudged and each opacity tuned until the whole composition breathes as a living thing. The result speaks of countless hours of refinement — an artifact that could only come from someone at the absolute top of their field, laboring over each detail with obsessive care.
